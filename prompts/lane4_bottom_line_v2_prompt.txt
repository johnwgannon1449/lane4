BOTTOM LINE V2 — VOICE STANDARD & RULES

JOB
3-4 sentences maximum. Every word earns its spot. Lead with what makes the school elite (specific, earned). Show pool fit with voice matching tier. End with directive that lands. No consolation language.

STRUCTURE: THREE BEATS

Beat 1: What makes this school elite (SPECIFIC, EARNED, NOT RANKINGS)
✅ "Johns Hopkins invented the American research university model and has never stopped."
✅ "Swarthmore is one of the three finest liberal arts colleges in the country."
✅ "MIT is the gold standard for engineering and science."
❌ "Johns Hopkins is located in Baltimore and was founded in 1876."
❌ "Swarthmore is in Pennsylvania."
❌ "MIT is ranked highly."

Beat 2: Pool truth with exact numbers, voice matching your tier (THIS IS CRITICAL)
Choose ONE voice register and stick with it throughout:

HIGH-POINT CONTENDER (swimmer belongs, is what coach is building around)
- "You're what they're building around"
- "You're a projected scorer at their conference championship"
- "You're a roster anchor"

SERIOUS RECRUIT (strong fit, coach will respond actively)
- "Expect genuine interest from this coaching staff"
- "You're a real asset to their roster"
- "This program will take you seriously"
- "You project as a conference scorer"

PRIORITY RECRUIT (good fit, noticeable advantage, solid spot)
- "You fit the roster need"
- "This is a realistic option"
- "You make this roster"
- "You project as a conference contributor"

RECRUITABLE (realistic path, contributor role)
- "You have a credible path here"
- "You project as a contributor at this level"
- "You have a real opportunity"

REACH (honest about difficulty, possible with support)
- "This is a reach, and that needs saying"
- "This is a reach, but not impossible with coach support"
- "Your times are below their typical recruits, but the coach supports swimmers who commit"

MAJOR REACH (call it out, no false hope)
- "This is a major reach"
- "This is a reach by any honest reading"

TIME DROP LANGUAGE RULE (CRITICAL)
ONLY mention time improvement if swimmer is BORDERLINE or REACH.
If swimmer is SERIOUS RECRUIT or PRIORITY, DO NOT say "a drop changes the conversation."
Example WRONG for Priority: "If he drops a second, this becomes more realistic."
Example RIGHT for Priority: Omit time drops entirely. They already belong.
Example RIGHT for Reach: "With a drop to 20.8, this becomes realistic."

Beat 3: Directive that lands (not consolation)
✅ "Get on the coach's radar now."
✅ "This is one of the best decisions he could make."
✅ "This one belongs on the visit list."
✅ "Don't overlook this one."
✅ "Let the times catch up and this opens up dramatically."
❌ "This is a genuine option."
❌ "This deserves a real look."
❌ "This is worth considering."
❌ "He has a real shot here."

DEAD PHRASES (NEVER USE)
❌ "deserves a real look"
❌ "deserves attention"
❌ "a worthwhile target"
❌ "genuine recruiting target"
❌ "don't let this one slip by" (standalone)
❌ "a genuine option"
❌ "this is a list-topper"
❌ "legitimate" (to describe fit or school)
❌ "approximately," "around," "estimated," "likely," "probably," "may"

NO HEDGE LANGUAGE
Never: "approximately," "around," "estimated," "likely," "may"
Always: exact numbers and direct language.

EXAMPLES THAT WORK

Johns Hopkins (Serious Recruit)
"Johns Hopkins is one of the handful of universities in the world where the name on your diploma opens doors in medicine, research, and biotech before you've written a cover letter. In the pool, Trystan is a legitimate 50 free threat in the Centennial, and Johns Hopkins is exactly the kind of program that notices. Get on the coach's radar now."

Swarthmore (Reach)
"Swarthmore is one of the three finest liberal arts colleges in the country, with a reputation that holds up in every room a graduate walks into. In the pool, Trystan sits below where Swarthmore typically recruits at the NESCAC level, where the 50 free regularly breaks 20 seconds. This is a reach, but not impossible if the coach supports your recruiting."

Carnegie Mellon (Major Reach)
"Carnegie Mellon is one of the most respected technical universities on the planet, and a degree here carries weight in every room it enters. In the pool, this is a major reach — the UAA program recruits at a higher level than your current times reflect. This is a moonshot, and that needs saying clearly."
