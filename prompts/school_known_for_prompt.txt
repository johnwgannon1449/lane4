EXAMPLES THAT WORK

Johns Hopkins:
"Johns Hopkins invented the American research university model, and it has never stopped operating that way. It is the top recipient of federal research funding in the country, year after year, and that money flows into labs that undergraduates actually work in. The medical school, the Bloomberg School of Public Health, and the Whiting School of Engineering are all world-class operations, and the undergraduate experience sits directly inside that ecosystem. This is not a school where research is a talking point; it is the actual culture of the place. Graduates move into medicine, research, biotech, and consulting at high rates, with the alumni base concentrated in the Mid-Atlantic, Boston, and the Bay Area."

Swarthmore:
"Swarthmore is one of the three finest liberal arts colleges in the country and operates at a level of intellectual rigor that matches much larger universities. The school is best known for producing thoughtful, engaged citizens who go on to leadership in law, medicine, policy, and academia. That reputation is earned through small classes, direct faculty relationships, and a student body that is genuinely curious. Outside of class, Swarthmore's Honor Code creates a social culture based on trust and mutual responsibility. The alumni network is active and engaged, particularly in the Northeast and Mid-Atlantic."

Rose-Hulman:
"Rose-Hulman is one of the finest engineering schools in the country and competes academically with much larger universities while maintaining a tight residential community. The school is known for producing engineers who are ready to work on day one — the curriculum is rigorous and hands-on from the start. That means lab work, design projects, and problem-solving that employers recognize immediately. The campus is contained and residential, which shapes a tight social community that students often credit as a differentiator compared to larger engineering schools. Graduates land at Cummins, Eli Lilly, Toyota, Boeing, and consulting firms, with the alumni base concentrated in the Midwest and strong connections to manufacturing and automotive industries."
