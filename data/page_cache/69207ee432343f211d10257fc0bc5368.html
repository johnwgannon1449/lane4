Our Best Investment Is You

We’re committed to making an Amherst education affordable.

At Amherst, we never want your financial situation to prevent you from receiving the education you deserve. Our generous financial aid policies and offers ensure that your degree is always within reach.

Financial Aid

Amherst + Affordability

What does it mean that Amherst is need-blind?

Our admission policy is need-blind for all applicants, domestic, international and undocumented. This means that our admission decision is based solely on your achievements, talents and promise. Your and your family's financial situation does not negatively impact your consideration for admission.
 

I’m a non-U.S. citizen. Can I be considered for admission to Amherst and financial aid?

Yes! Amherst College welcomes first-year and transfer applications from international students, including those under DACA, undocumented individuals, those with fragile citizenship, and non-citizen permanent residents. About 10 percent of students enrolled at Amherst are international students. We provide support services through our International Students Office. Amherst is one of the very few U.S. colleges to offer need-blind financial aid for international students, just as we do for domestic students. “Need-blind” means that, when evaluating your application, Amherst will not take into account whether you have applied for financial aid.

Is financial aid available for transfer students?

Yes. We treat transfer applicants just like first-year applicants. You are considered for admission on a need-blind basis and your calculated need is met in full.

Three Steps to Support Your Exploration

Image

Your Amherst journey starts today!

  1. Start by familiarizing yourself with everything Amherst has to offer. Explore our majors, student organizations, career paths, and more.
  2. Visit campus, either in person or virtually. Take a tour in person or explore virtually to get a feel of what the next four years could look like. Connect with students and alumni to learn more about their college experience.
  3. It’s time to apply! Make sure to submit your application and FAFSA on time. Deadlines will depend on your student status and whether or not you’d like to be considered for early decision.

Timeline and Important Dates

Enrollment for First-Year Students 

  • Early decision: Please submit application materials by November 7, 2025; financial aid applications are due by November 14, 2025.
  • Regular decision: Please submit application materials by January 5, 2026; financial aid applications are due by January 15, 2026.

Enrollment for Transfer Students

  • Spring enrollment: Please submit application and financial aid materials by November 3, 2025
  • Fall enrollment: Please submit application and financial aid materials by March 2, 2026
Application Checklist
  • Secondary school transcript
  • Teacher and counselor recommendations
  • Quality of writing as demonstrated in your essays, writing supplement, testing and recommendations
  • Extra- and co-curricular involvements, talents, and leadership
  • Standardized test results (optional / if submitted by student)
