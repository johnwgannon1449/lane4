About Amherst

Amherst College prepares students to make a difference in the world. Our financial aid program is among the most substantial in the nation, and our student body is among the most diverse. At Amherst, you are part of a lively, inquisitive, devoted community of students, faculty, staff, and alumni who inspire and support one another.

Intellectual, Supportive, Transformative

Image

Since our 1821 founding, Amherst has demonstrated the value of the liberal arts and the importance of critical thinking. Today, our mission is more imperative than ever, and we’re prepared to meet the challenge.

Small classes, an open curriculum, and a singular focus on undergraduate education ensure that our faculty engage and challenge our students every day. It’s personal. An Amherst education is rigorous but supportive, offering individual guidance from professors who are leading scholars and dedicated mentors.

And life on our campus is an adventure in itself. Tons of events, over a hundred student organizations, lots of places to hang out and meet friends, and all kinds of support help our students flourish and know that they belong.

The result of an Amherst education? Our graduates are equipped not only to thrive in an increasingly global and complex world, but to enjoy a lifetime of connection and learning. 

Where is Amherst College? We’re in the town of Amherst, Massachusetts. Come for a visit and explore our beautiful, quintessentially New England campus.

Celebrating 50 Years of Coeducation

Beginning in 2026 and extending through 2027, we are celebrating one of our most meaningful milestones: the 50th anniversary of coeducation at Amherst. We will honor the trailblazers who pushed coeducation forward, and the subsequent generations of women who added new chapters to the story of Amherst.

After Amherst Alumni & Parent Programs

Attractions

The Powerhouse

Amherst’s own nightclub — a renovated steam plant — is a popular spot for live performances, movie screenings, art exhibits, panel discussions, pub nights, outdoor picnics, and more.

The Powerhouse

Beneski Museum of Natural History

Explore three floors of exhibits and thousands of specimens, including the world’s largest collection of dinosaur tracks and North America’s first confirmed evidence of a dinosaur.

Beneski Museum

Mead Art Museum

The College’s own art museum contains more than 19,000 objects spanning 5,000 years of global culture. Students come here to view the latest exhibitions, see favorite artworks, take classes, and meet friends.

The Mead

The Emily Dickinson Museum

Visit the home of poet Emily Dickinson and see the bedroom in which she wrote by lamplight.

Emily Dickinson Museum

Wildlife Sanctuary

Explore 500 acres of open fields, wetlands, woods, and ponds—without ever leaving campus. Highlights include the Fort River, a trail system, and a variety of wild friends, including downy woodpeckers, turtles, and foxes.

Explore Our 500 Acres

Book & Plow Farm

Our own 20-acre farm was conceived by students, who work the farm (along with dedicated staff) to grow vegetables for the dining hall, a CSA, and for donation to local food pantries.

Explore the farm

Memorial Hill

Living on campus means seeing breathtaking views of the Mount Holyoke Range every day. Students come to Memorial Hill to take in the view, reflect, relax, and connect.

Kirby Theater

Kirby Memorial Theater is a state-of-the-art performance venue with 384 seats and computerized lighting and sound equipment. Just behind Kirby is the Holden Experimental Theater.

Amherst College Theaters

Johnson Chapel

Dedicated in 1827, Johnson Chapel is home to many of the College’s most prominent events, as well as the English department and film and media studies program. The chapel walls are lined with portraits of Amherst presidents, alumni, and others.

Johnson Chapel Portraits

Yushien Japanese Garden

Nestled between Webster Hall and Kirby Theater is our Japanese garden, with carefully tended stone steps, trees, and flowering shrubs. The Yushien (roughly translated as “Garden of Friendship”) celebrates the strong ties between Amherst College and Doshisha University in Japan.

Our Town & Area

The town of Amherst is home to a vibrant community of 35,000 people. More than 30,000 college students live in Amherst during the academic year. The walkable downtown is filled with restaurants, coffee houses, a movie theater, and a live-music venue.

Town & Valley

Events

There’s always something fun to do at Amherst! We host gatherings, speakers, exhibitions, athletic events, performances, and more throughout the year. Our seasonal festivals offer snacks, games, and performances, including by the Zumbyes singing group, shown. (Yes, that is a banana costume.)

Event Calendar

Diversity, Equity & Inclusion

Amherst is proud of its work to recruit, admit, and enroll a diverse student body. We’re dedicated to creating a sense of belonging in our close-knit community. 

Diversity & Inclusion

Sustainability

Sustainability is a core principle that guides all of our operations—from the food served (and grown on our campus farm) to the vehicles driven. With our Climate Action Plan, we aim to achieve climate neutrality by 2030.

Sustainability Climate Action Plan

Amherst History

For over two centuries, Amherst has been educating a talented, diverse student body. See Amherst’s historical “firsts,” visit our Bicentennial pages, and more.

Amherst History
