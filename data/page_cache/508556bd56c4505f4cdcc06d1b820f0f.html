Junior Preview Saturdays

As you begin your college search, we invite you to take an early look at Hamilton. You are invited to join us on campus in April.

Register Now to Attend

Admitted Student Events

Events for students admitted to the Class of 2030 are planned for Monday, April 13 and Monday, April 20. These will be day-long events and will include presentations, panels, a campus tour, a community lunch, and opportunities for students to meet with other Hamilton community members and celebrate their admission to the Class of 2030.

 

Smaller celebratory visit opportunities will also be available on Saturday, April 11 and Saturday, April 18. These Saturday visits will include a campus tour, a panel discussion with current students, and opportunity to have lunch in a dining hall.

 

Registration for all admitted student events will open in late March, and admitted students will be contacted via email with a link to register.

Personal Interviews

An interview will not only provide us with a strong sense of your personal strengths and interests that may not be obvious in an application, it will also give you a chance to ask questions and learn more about Hamilton in a personal setting.

Learn More About Interviews

Visit a Class

When classes are in session, prospective students who are visitors of the Admission Office are generally welcome to sit in on classes. We encourage you to arrive five to ten minutes before the class begins to introduce yourself to the professor and to make sure the content for that day is appropriate (not an exam day, etc.).

Virtual Information Session

Video information sessions are held once per month, on select Wednesdays. Led by an admission dean, these 30 minute sessions provide an opportunity to learn more about the College, explore Hamilton’s Promise, and have your questions answered.

Register for a Virtual Information Session

Virtually Hamilton Events

From exploring our community to details about the application and financial aid process, you’ll hear from Admission Officers, students, faculty, and other Hamilton community members in these online sessions throughout the summer and fall.

Register for a Virtually Hamilton Event

More Information about Campus Visits

Should you require assistance in scheduling your campus visit, please reach out to Courtney Combs, Admission Visit Coordinator, at ccombs@hamilton.edu.


If you need financial assistance to visit campus, we offer limited travel reimbursement on a competitive, ad hoc basis to subsidize a portion of the costs associated with visiting campus for high school seniors who wouldn’t otherwise have the opportunity to do so. For more information, please contact Rachel Clarey, Assistant Dean of Admission, at rclarey@hamilton.edu.
