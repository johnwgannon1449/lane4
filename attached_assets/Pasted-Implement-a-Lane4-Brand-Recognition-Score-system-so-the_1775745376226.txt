Implement a **Lane4 Brand Recognition Score** system so the onboarding reveal and early Explore results prioritize recognizable, exciting schools when the swimmer is eligible.

Do NOT change swim scoring, conference scoring logic, or recruiting likelihood math.
This is a **presentation-layer ranking** used only when multiple schools are eligible for the swimmer.

---

PURPOSE

---

Lane4’s first reveal should answer:

“What is the best-known school I could realistically swim at?”

When the system identifies the pool of schools where a swimmer’s times fit, it should prefer schools with stronger brand recognition when selecting the **Top 6 reveal** and ordering early Explore results.

This does not override swim eligibility.
It only **prioritizes recognizable schools when swim scores are similar.**

---

IMPLEMENTATION

---

Add a new field to the school data model:

brand_rank

Lower number = stronger brand recognition.

Example:

Harvard → brand_rank: 1
Whitman → brand_rank: 110+

When selecting Top 6 schools:

1. Filter schools where swimmer has a legitimate swim fit.
2. Sort by brand_rank (ascending).
3. Allow up to **3 stretch schools** if swim scores are within the acceptable stretch range.
4. Fill the Top 6 with the highest brand schools from the eligible pool.

Goal: If a swimmer can realistically swim at a recognizable program, it should appear in the first reveal.
