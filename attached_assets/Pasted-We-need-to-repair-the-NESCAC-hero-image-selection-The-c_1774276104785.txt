We need to repair the NESCAC hero image selection. The current results include:
- wrong school images (Amherst, Connecticut College)
- black-and-white / historical photos
- unclear or low-signal shots

We are NOT switching to manual filenames yet. We are improving the selection system and re-running only the weak schools.

==================================================
GOAL
==================================================

Re-run hero image selection ONLY for schools with poor results and produce high-quality, school-accurate, modern images.

==================================================
INPUT
==================================================

Use:
- nescac_hero_manifest.json

Focus ONLY on schools with weak images:
- Amherst College
- Connecticut College
- any others previously flagged as poor (historical, unclear, wrong)

==================================================
STEP 1 — FETCH CANDIDATES
==================================================

For each target school:
1. Use the `hero_query`
2. Retrieve 5–10 candidate image URLs
3. Include:
   - official school/admissions pages (highest priority)
   - athletics or news pages (secondary)
   - Wikimedia (allowed but lower priority)

==================================================
STEP 2 — APPLY STRICT FILTERING
==================================================

Reject any candidate that:
- is black-and-white
- appears historical/archival
- is low resolution
- is an aerial unless clearly iconic
- is too zoomed out / unclear subject
- does not clearly match the school
- looks like a generic campus not tied to the school

==================================================
STEP 3 — PRIORITY RULES
==================================================

Rank candidates by:

1. Official school (.edu) source with clear campus shot
2. Recognizable landmark tied to the school
3. Strong composition (clear, centered subject, good lighting)
4. Modern color image
5. Horizontal orientation preferred

==================================================
STEP 4 — SCHOOL-SPECIFIC ANCHORS
==================================================

Apply these constraints:

Amherst College:
- strongly prefer Johnson Chapel / main quad (Academic Quad)
- must clearly look like Amherst’s campus

Connecticut College:
- prefer arboretum, coastal feel, or main campus green
- must NOT look generic or like another school

==================================================
STEP 5 — SELECTION RULE
==================================================

From filtered candidates:
- select the single best image
- if NO candidate meets quality standards, SKIP the school (do not assign a bad image)

==================================================
STEP 6 — OUTPUT
==================================================

Update or create:
data/school_images.json

Format:
{
  "Amherst College": {
    "hero": "FINAL_URL"
  }
}

==================================================
STEP 7 — DEBUG OUTPUT (REQUIRED)
==================================================

For EACH processed school, show:

- candidate URLs considered (list)
- which ones were rejected and why
- final selected image URL
- explanation of why it was chosen

Also list any skipped schools.

==================================================
SUCCESS CRITERIA
==================================================

- Amherst image clearly shows Johnson Chapel / main quad
- Connecticut College image clearly represents that campus (not generic)
- no black-and-white or historical images
- no obviously wrong schools
- images feel modern, clear, and recognizable
