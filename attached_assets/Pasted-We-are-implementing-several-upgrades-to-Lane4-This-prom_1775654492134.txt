## PART 4 — EVENT STRENGTH USING "NUMBER OF As"

Convert swimmer time to a continuous A-score.

Definition:

0A = B time
1A = A time
2A = AA time
3A = AAA time
4A = AAAA time

Compute fractional position between levels.

Example:

If swimmer is halfway between AA and AAA:

score = 2.5A

If faster than AAAA:

score > 4A

Algorithm:

1. Identify which two standards the swimmer time falls between.
2. Compute fractional distance between them.
3. Convert to A-score.

Example:

AA = 1:58
AAA = 1:50

Swimmer = 1:54

score = 2 + (AA_time - swimmer_time)/(AA_time - AAA_time)

Store the computed A-score internally.
