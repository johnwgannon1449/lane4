Update the Lane4 **Top 6 onboarding reveal logic** so the first schools shown feel recognizable, exciting, and credible.

Do NOT change the swim scoring engine, recruiting likelihood calculations, or school ranking math.
This change only affects **which schools are chosen for the initial Top 6 reveal.**

---

GOAL

---

The first six schools a swimmer sees should feel exciting and believable.

Whenever possible, the Top 6 should consist of **recognizable schools that are realistic swim options**, with a few exciting stretch schools included only when they are genuinely plausible.

The goal is for the swimmer to immediately think:

"I know that school. I could swim there."

---

TOP 6 SELECTION RULES

---

When building the Top 6 reveal:

1. Start with all schools where the swimmer has a legitimate swim fit according to the current scoring model.

2. From this set, prioritize schools with **strong brand recognition** when swim scores are similar.

Examples of recognizable schools include:

• strong academic brands
• well-known D3 programs
• recognizable regional universities
• schools swimmers frequently discuss during recruiting

3. The Top 6 should ideally include:

• **3 realistic swim fits**
Schools where the swimmer has strong conference scoring potential.

• **up to 3 stretch options**
Schools that are slightly faster but still within a believable range.

4. Stretch schools should only appear if they are **genuinely exciting or recognizable**.

Examples:

A well-known program
A highly ranked academic school
A historically strong swim program

Do NOT include obscure schools as stretch options.

If exciting stretch schools do not exist, fill remaining slots with additional realistic fits instead.

---

CARD CONTENT

---

Each Top 6 school card should include:

School Name
Swim Potential Badge (Podium Potential / A Final Potential / Conference Scorer etc.)
School Tagline (already generated elsewhere in the system)
Location
School Image if available

---

IMPORTANT PRINCIPLE

---

The Top 6 list is a **curated reveal moment**, not a strict ranking.

When multiple schools have similar swim-fit scores, always prefer the school that a swimmer is more likely to recognize or feel excited about.

This improves engagement while remaining honest to the swim data.

---

GOAL EXPERIENCE

---

The swimmer should see the Top 6 and immediately recognize at least a few schools and feel motivated to explore further.
