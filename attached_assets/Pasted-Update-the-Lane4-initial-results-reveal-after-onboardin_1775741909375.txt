Update the Lane4 **initial results reveal** (after onboarding) so the first screen feels exciting, credible, and swimmer-focused.

Do not change the recruiting engine or scoring calculations.
This is a **presentation and selection layer change only.**

---

SECTION TITLE

---

Change the results header from:

Your Lane Assignment

to:

Best College Swim Fits

Subtext:

Based on your times, these programs look like strong fits.

---

TOP 6 SCHOOL SELECTION LOGIC

---

The current system appears to simply take the top 6 by swim score.

Instead, create a **balanced reveal that prioritizes recognizable schools when possible.**

Selection priorities should be:

1. Strong swim fit
2. Recognizable academic brands (when available)
3. Schools where swimmer could score at conference meet
4. Schools where swimmer could reach A-final or podium potential

Suggested reveal composition:

• 2 strong swim fits
• 2 recognizable academic schools
• 1 stretch / exciting school
• 1 additional realistic option

Do NOT sacrifice realism — but when multiple schools have similar swim scores, prefer the one with **higher brand recognition.**

---

CARD CONTENT

---

Each of the 6 reveal cards should now include:

1. School Name

2. Swim Potential Badge

Replace generic labels with swimmer-relevant tags such as:

Conference Meet Scorer
A Final Potential
Podium Potential
Strong Swim Fit
Coach Interest Possible
Longer-Term Swim Goal

These should come from existing scoring logic where possible.

3. School Tagline

Use the **existing generated school tagline** that already appears in the Explore cards.

Example style:

"Top-tier academics with one of the fastest rising swim programs in the conference."

or

"Elite liberal arts college where smart swimmers thrive."

4. School Image

If a curated school image exists in the image system:

Display the hero campus image on the card.

If no image exists:

Display the card cleanly without a broken placeholder.

---

CARD LAYOUT

---

Each reveal card should contain:

School Photo (if available)

School Name

Swim Potential Badge

School Tagline

Cards should feel visually richer than the current simple list.

---

VISUAL TONE

---

This screen should feel like a **big reveal moment**, not a database result.

Design cues:

• slightly larger cards
• clear swim potential badges
• concise tagline
• recognizable school names emphasized

---

TRANSITION TO EXPLORE

---

Below the Top 6 reveal, keep the button:

Explore All My Schools →

When clicked, transition into the full Explore interface.

However, do NOT drop the user directly into a blank search bar.

Instead show:

More Schools That Fit Your Times

with additional personalized school cards before the search UI.

---

IMPORTANT GOAL

---

The first reveal should make a swimmer immediately think:

"Wow, I could actually swim there."

Recognizable schools, clear swim potential, and the existing school taglines should make the results feel exciting and credible.
