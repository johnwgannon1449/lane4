We are still getting too many stock-looking images in the Lane4 image curation pipeline.

This means the current collector is too broad and is pulling generic marketing/admissions lifestyle photos instead of school-specific campus and swim imagery.

We need to tighten the sourcing and ranking logic immediately.

GOAL
Return 8–20 candidate images from official school sources that are strong, school-specific, and visually aspirational.
We want:
- campus/place images
- iconic architecture
- quad/aerial/outdoor setting
- swim facility / natatorium / pool images
We do NOT want generic brochure-style stock-looking student photos.

==================================================
IMPORTANT CHANGE
==================================================

Do not treat all images on official school websites as equally valid.

We need to rank for PLACE and FACILITY, not generic marketing people imagery.

==================================================
SOURCE PRIORITY
==================================================

Only crawl a small set of high-value official page types:

Highest priority:
- university homepage
- campus visit pages
- campus life overview pages
- about campus pages
- traditions / landmarks pages
- athletics homepage
- swimming and diving pages
- aquatics / natatorium / facilities pages

De-prioritize heavily or skip:
- student support pages
- generic admissions subpages
- news posts unless clearly campus/facility related
- faculty/staff pages
- student profile pages
- fundraising/giving pages
- campaign pages
- diversity brochure pages
- pages dominated by generic student marketing photography

==================================================
IMAGE RANKING RULES
==================================================

For HERO candidates, prefer images that are:
- large
- landscape orientation
- outdoor
- campus-first
- iconic architecture or wide campus scene
- visually clear even at thumbnail size
- colorful / vibrant
- not cluttered

For POOL candidates, prefer:
- natatorium
- pool deck
- lane lines / blocks
- swimming facility interior/exterior
- official athletics/facility imagery

For STUDENT LIFE candidates, only keep if they feel authentic and strong.
Do not force generic smiling student photos into the candidate set.

==================================================
NEGATIVE FILTERS
==================================================

Aggressively penalize or reject images that look like:
- posed close-up students smiling at camera
- classroom discussion table scenes
- laptop-on-grass brochure shots
- generic marketing lifestyle imagery
- handshake / advising / lecture closeups
- headshots
- tiny promo banners
- text-overlay assets
- stock-photo-looking diversity brochure images
- logos
- icons
- seals
- maps
- SVGs
- repeated duplicates

==================================================
SOURCE PAGE KEYWORD BOOSTING
==================================================

Boost images whose SOURCE PAGE URL or title contains:
- visit
- campus
- about
- traditions
- landmarks
- student-life
- facilities
- aquatics
- natatorium
- swimming

Penalize images whose SOURCE PAGE URL or title contains:
- support
- advising
- diversity
- profile
- apply
- campaign
- giving
- news
- faculty

==================================================
IMPORTANT IMPLEMENTATION DETAIL
==================================================

Do not just scrape all img tags from official pages and rank by size.

We need page-aware candidate selection:
- collect source page metadata
- attach each candidate image to the page it came from
- use page type as part of ranking
- prefer campus/facility pages
- de-prioritize brochure-style marketing pages

==================================================
OPTIONAL IMPROVEMENT
==================================================

Internally bucket candidates before display:
- Campus / Hero-leaning
- Pool / Swim-leaning
- Student-life-leaning

The user-facing selection UI can still stay simple:
1st selected = Hero
2nd selected = Pool
3rd selected = Student Life
But candidate generation should be smarter behind the scenes.

==================================================
SUCCESS CRITERIA
==================================================

When I review candidate images for a school, I should mostly see:
- campus scenes
- architecture
- iconic views
- pool/facility shots

I should NOT mostly see generic marketing/student brochure photos.
