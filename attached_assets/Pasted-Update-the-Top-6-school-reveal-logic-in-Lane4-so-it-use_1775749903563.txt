Update the **Top 6 school reveal logic** in Lane4 so it uses the existing **Recruiting Likelihood tiers** instead of the previous swim-fit (adjPts) logic.

Do NOT change any scoring math, conference projections, or recruiting tier calculations.
This change only affects **how the Top 6 schools are selected and sorted for the onboarding reveal.**

---

GOAL

---

Lane4’s first reveal should answer:

**“What are the best known schools where I could realistically be recruited?”**

The selection should rely on the already-computed **Recruiting Likelihood tiers** rather than the older swim-fit system.

This aligns the reveal with Lane4’s philosophy:

• honest recruiting expectations
• no rainbow chasing
• focus on realistic opportunities

---

STEP 1 — BUILD ELIGIBLE SCHOOL POOL

---

From the full set of schools evaluated for the swimmer:

Exclude any school where:

Recruiting Likelihood = **NOT COMPETITIVE**

These schools should **never appear in the Top 6 reveal**.

They may still appear deeper in Explore if a user searches intentionally.

Remaining eligible tiers are:

• Competitive
• Possible
• Reach

---

STEP 2 — CLASSIFY SCHOOLS

---

Group the eligible schools into:

REALISTIC FITS
Recruiting Likelihood = **Competitive** or **Possible**

STRETCH FITS
Recruiting Likelihood = **Reach**

---

STEP 3 — SORT WITHIN EACH GROUP

---

Sort both groups using the Lane4 brand recognition ranking.

Primary sort:

brand_rank (ascending — lower number = stronger brand)

Tie-breakers:

projected_conference_finish (ascending)
swim_score (descending)

---

STEP 4 — BUILD TOP 6 REVEAL

---

Construct the Top 6 using this order:

1. Fill with **Competitive / Possible** schools first
2. If fewer than 6 exist, fill remaining slots with **Reach** schools
3. Maximum **3 Reach schools** in the Top 6
4. Prefer the highest **brand_rank** schools when multiple options exist

This ensures the reveal contains recognizable schools while remaining honest about recruiting likelihood.

---

STEP 5 — CARD BADGES

---

Display swim potential badges based on recruiting likelihood:

Competitive → Conference Scorer / A-Final Potential
Possible → Strong Swim Fit
Reach → Longer-Term Swim Goal

Do NOT display NOT COMPETITIVE schools in this reveal.

---

EXPECTED RESULT

---

The swimmer should see something like:

Best College Swim Fits

Johns Hopkins — Conference Scorer
Carnegie Mellon — A Final Potential
Case Western — Conference Scorer
Tufts — Strong Swim Fit
Emory — Longer-Term Swim Goal
Williams — Longer-Term Swim Goal

This creates an exciting but honest reveal that prioritizes recognizable schools where the swimmer has legitimate recruiting potential.
