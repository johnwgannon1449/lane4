Implement a new **first-time onboarding flow** for Lane4 designed to immediately hook swimmers by pulling their best times, showing fast value, and only asking for email after demonstrating usefulness.

This flow should feel **fun, swimmer-native, and credible**, not gimmicky or corporate.

Do not modify the recruiting engine logic. This is primarily a **frontend onboarding and orchestration change**.

---

GLOBAL AUTH BEHAVIOR

---

If a user is already logged in:

• Do NOT show onboarding
• Send them directly to their normal logged-in landing page (dashboard/results)

If a user is NOT logged in:

• Show the new onboarding hero

Session persistence should keep users logged in normally between visits.

Add a visible **Log In** link for returning users on the hero screen.

---

SCREEN 1 — HERO

Headline:

Let’s get your college lane assignment.

Subtext:

Enter your name and I’ll find your best times.

Input field:

Swimmer Name

Primary Button:

Find My Times

Secondary link (small but visible):

Already have an account? Log in

---

SCREEN 2 — SWIMMER MATCH

Search public swim results and return possible swimmer matches.

If multiple matches appear:

Header:

Which one is you?

Display cards showing:

Name
Club / Team
Grad Year
Location (if available)

User selects their correct profile.

---

SCREEN 3 — FETCHING TIMES

Do NOT show a spinner.

Show rotating swim-themed loading messages.

Rotate every ~1.5 seconds.

Example messages:

Grabbing best times…
Checking the psych sheet…
Dang, that’s moving.
Lane 4 energy detected.
Counting strokes, not vibes.
Trying not to get split-swindled…
Yep, those are real swimmer times.
Sorting the pain-cave events…
Hold on, verifying that 100 free…
Someone’s been putting in yardage.

---

SCREEN 4 — TIME VALIDATION

Header:

These look like your best times — good to go?

Display imported PRs.

Allow swimmer to:

• confirm times
• edit times
• delete events
• optionally add missing events

Primary Button:

Looks Good

---

SCREEN 5 — SEARCHING FOR COLLEGES

Once the swimmer confirms times, immediately begin a real background search for colleges where those times align.

Show rotating loading messages again.

Examples:

Visiting campuses for you…
Looking for schools where you can actually contribute…
Trying not to send you rainbow chasing…
Scanning for smart swimmer schools…
Somewhere, a coach just nodded quietly.
Finding the sweet spot between fast and feasible…
Grandma would definitely brag about this place…
Checking where stopwatch and transcript can coexist.

---

SCREEN 6 — MATCH COUNT REVEAL

Before asking for email, reveal how many colleges were found.

Use the following buckets:

0 schools
"Looks like you're still improving toward college recruiting range. Want to explore schools while you keep training?"

1–4 schools
"We found a few colleges where your times line up."

5–11 schools
"We found several colleges where your times line up."

12–24 schools
"We found a grip of colleges where your times line up."

25+ schools
"We found dozens of colleges where your times line up."

---

SCREEN 7 — EMAIL CAPTURE

Only now ask for email.

Example:

Headline:

We found dozens of colleges where your times fit.

Subtext:

Drop your email and I’ll save your results and show your best-fit schools.

Input:

Email Address

Button:

Show My Lane Assignment

This step should create the new user account and attach the imported swimmer data.

---

SCREEN 8 — BUILDING RESULTS

While the system prepares the results page, show one more rotating message set.

Examples:

Checking conference scoring tables…
Comparing your events across programs…
Looking for teams where you can score…
Trying not to send you to fantasyland…
Almost there… lining up your lane assignment.

---

SCREEN 9 — RESULTS

Show the swimmer their **Top 6 Lane Assignments**.

These should be schools where their current swim times align well with the program.

Sort results to include recognizable academic brands where possible **without compromising realism**.

Do NOT show admissions likelihood yet (academics not collected).

Instead show swim-focused indicators such as:

Strong Swim Fit
Worth Exploring
Coach Interest Possible
Longer-Term Swim Goal

---

DESIGN NOTES

• Keep screens clean and fast
• Avoid heavy animations
• Maintain a confident but playful tone
• Do not overdo humor
• The first meaningful payoff should happen within 20–30 seconds

Goal:

A swimmer should enter their name and quickly feel:

"This thing actually knows where I might swim."
